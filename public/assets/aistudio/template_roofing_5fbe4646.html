Truth In Roofing Warranties

Roofing Warranties In Houston: Why The Number Isn't The Point

You'll hear a lot about 30-year, 40-year, even 50-year products and warranties. Sounds impressive. But in Houston, that number usually isn't what determines a roof's lifecycle or when it needs replacement.

Most roofs here are replaced because of insurance requirements, age thresholds, or storm history—not because the warranty ran out.

What You Hear What Actually Matters
“50-Year Warranty” Most roofs are replaced long before that due to weather & insurance rules
“Lifetime Coverage” Only applies if the flashing, ventilation, and install are done 100% to spec
“You're Fully Protected” Only if the warranty is properly registered by a certified contractor

We offer strong manufacturer-backed warranties, including GAF Golden Pledge® options. Focus remains on getting the install right, registering every document properly, and choosing coverage that fits your ownership timeline.
