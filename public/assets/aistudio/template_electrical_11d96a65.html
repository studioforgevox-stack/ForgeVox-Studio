Frequently Asked Electrical Questions

How can I improve the safety of my home's electrical system? ▼

Safety starts with regular inspections, whole-home surge protection, upgrading obsolete fuse panels to modern 200A breaker systems, and making sure all wet areas (kitchens, bathrooms, exteriors) are equipped with GFCI/RCD protection.

What is the typical cost for residential electrical repairs? ▼

Costs vary depending on accessibility, parts, and whether dedicated circuits are needed. We provide transparent upfront quotes with zero hidden fees before starting any work.

Do you offer a warranty on electrical panel replacements? ▼

Yes. In addition to our 100% Money-Back Satisfaction Guarantee, we provide an industry-leading lifetime workmanship warranty on all main breaker panel replacements and service upgrades.
